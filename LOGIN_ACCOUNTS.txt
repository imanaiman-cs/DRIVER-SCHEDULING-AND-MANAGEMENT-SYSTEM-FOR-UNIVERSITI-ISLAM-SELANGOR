NOTES
  - These are demo accounts created by importing database.sql.
  - The first successful sign-in upgrades the stored password to a stronger hash.
  - 5 wrong passwords in a row block the account for 15 minutes; it unblocks by itself
    (nothing to unlock manually). For a local test you may clear it with:
        UPDATE users SET locked_until = NULL, failed_attempts = 0 WHERE username = 'admin';
  - Sessions end after 15 minutes without activity.
  - Change all passwords before any real use of the system.
